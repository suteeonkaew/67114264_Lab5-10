1. The Convoy Effect:
In FCFS, P2 and P3 waited 10 and 12 time units because P1 ran first for 10 units. This shows the convoy effect: short jobs wait behind a long job. Round Robin reduced average waiting time from 7.33 to 4.33 units.

2. Choosing the Time Quantum:
A very large quantum makes Round Robin behave like FCFS because each process can finish in one turn. A very small quantum causes frequent context switches, increasing overhead and reducing efficiency.

3. AI Context:
